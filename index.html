01

Remote sensing · Machine learning · Google Earth Engine

Land Cover Classification

Know exactly what's on the ground, without months of field surveys.

The challenge
Planners and environmental teams need accurate, up-to-date maps of built-up areas, vegetation, water and bare land, but ground surveys are slow, costly and quickly out of date.
What I built
A supervised machine-learning workflow in Google Earth Engine: image preprocessing, carefully designed training samples, model training and accuracy checks, then GIS-ready land-cover layers.
Your advantage
A map you can trust for zoning, tracking urban growth, protecting habitats and reporting, repeatable for any year or region.

Time-Series Forecasting

See demand, sales or climate trends coming before they arrive.

A forecasting model using the XGBoost gradient-boosting algorithm, trained on historical time-series data to capture trend and seasonality and improve accuracy over simple methods.

MSc thesis · Swansea University · 2025/26 Chevening

Comparing Summer Urban Surface Heat, Population Exposure and Environmental Drivers across Greater London and Birmingham–West Midlands (MSOAs), 2021–2025

Where do cities get hottest in summer, who lives in those hotspots, and which environmental factors are driving the heat? This study compared two of England's largest urban regions, neighbourhood by neighbourhood, across five summers.
